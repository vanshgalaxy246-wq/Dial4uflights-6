The city

Why Marrakech?

Marrakech draws UK travellers with its medina, riads, souks, rooftop views and easy access to the Atlas Mountains. The city’s atmosphere — from Jemaa el-Fnaa to quiet courtyard stays — makes the flight from London or other UK airports a popular choice for short breaks and longer escapes.

Whether you come for culture, food or a change of pace, the journey begins with the right flight from the UK.
