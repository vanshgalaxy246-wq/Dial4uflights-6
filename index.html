Flights from London to Faro

London to Faro flights are one of the most popular routes for UK travellers heading to Portugal’s Algarve region. Faro Airport (FAO) serves as the main gateway to the southern Portuguese coast, giving easy access to historic Faro itself as well as the wider Algarve.

Whether you are planning a beach holiday, a short city break or a longer stay exploring the region, understanding the route options helps you choose the most suitable itinerary. Direct flights operate regularly from several London airports, with additional connecting options available when needed.

When comparing flights from London to Faro, consider departure airport, total journey time, baggage allowance and cabin class. Demand is strongest during the summer months and UK school holidays, so early planning often provides a wider choice of schedules and fare types.

London Airports for Faro Flights

Several London airports handle flights to Faro (FAO). Availability changes by season, airline and travel date, so it is useful to check more than one departure point.

London Heathrow (LHR)

Heathrow offers scheduled services to Faro with British Airways on many dates. Convenient for travellers in west or central London and those connecting from other cities.

London Gatwick (LGW)

Gatwick is one of the busiest airports for Faro flights, with frequent services from easyJet, British Airways and others. A practical choice for many passengers in south London and surrounding areas.

London Stansted (STN)

Stansted is regularly used by leisure and low-cost carriers serving Faro. Direct seasonal and year-round options have been available depending on the airline programme.

London Luton (LTN)

Luton also features regularly in Faro schedules. It can be a convenient option depending on where you live and the specific flight times available.

Because schedules change, always verify the current departure airport for your preferred dates. Comparing total door-to-door time often matters as much as the scheduled flight duration.

Faro Airport (FAO)

Faro Airport (IATA: FAO) is the main international airport for the Algarve. It is located a short distance west of Faro city centre — typically around 4–10 km depending on the exact destination within the city.

Under normal conditions the road transfer to Faro city centre takes approximately 10–15 minutes. The airport also serves as the primary arrival point for travellers heading to other Algarve destinations such as Albufeira, Vilamoura, Lagos, Portimão and Tavira.

Getting from FAO to your accommodation

  • Taxi or private transfer — the most straightforward door-to-door option, especially with luggage or for late arrivals.
  • Public bus — regular services operate between the airport and Faro city; further connections are available to other Algarve towns.
  • Car hire — available at the airport for travellers who prefer independent mobility around the region.

Planning your transfer in advance is advisable during peak summer periods when demand is highest.

Cheap Flights from London to Faro

Travellers looking for cheap flights from London to Faro can improve their chances of finding suitable fares by taking a flexible approach to dates, airports and itinerary type.

Useful steps include:

  • Comparing prices across Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted and Luton for the same travel window.
  • Checking both mid-week and weekend departures — leisure demand often raises weekend fares.
  • Looking at shoulder periods either side of the main school holiday peaks.
  • Weighing non-stop flights against one-stop connections when total journey time and price are considered together.
  • Reviewing baggage rules carefully — a low base fare can become less competitive once checked bags are added.
  • Comparing the full cost of one-way versus return tickets under the same conditions.

Smart fare tip: Focus on the total price including the baggage you actually need, plus realistic transfer time at FAO, rather than the headline fare alone.

Direct Flights from London to Faro

Direct (non-stop) flights from London to Faro operate regularly. Capacity is generally highest during the summer months when leisure demand for the Algarve peaks, but year-round services are also available on many routes.

Airlines and exact frequencies vary by departure airport and season. Direct services have been offered from Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted and Luton depending on the airline programme and time of year.

Typical non-stop flight time is approximately 2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours 10 minutes. Always confirm the current schedule for your specific dates, as programmes are subject to change.

London to Faro Flight Time

A typical non-stop London to Faro flight lasts around three hours. Scheduled block times commonly fall between 2 hours 45 minutes and 3 hours 10 minutes, depending on departure airport, aircraft type and winds.

Connecting flights add the duration of the layover plus any extra flying time on the second sector. When evaluating options, look at total journey time, minimum connection time, whether bags are checked through to FAO, and arrival time at the airport.

One-Way and Return Flights from London to Faro

Both one-way and return flights from London to Faro are available. Many holidaymakers book a return ticket for simplicity and, in some cases, better overall value. Others prefer the flexibility of separate one-way tickets.

Key points to compare: total price of a return versus two one-way tickets, baggage allowance on each sector, change and cancellation rules, and whether the outbound and inbound flights are with the same airline or partners.

When to Travel to Faro

Spring

Mild temperatures, fewer crowds and pleasant conditions for exploring Faro Old Town and the Ria Formosa. A good shoulder season for many travellers.

Summer

Peak season with the warmest weather and busiest beaches. Highest flight demand and the widest range of schedules. Book earlier for popular dates.

Autumn

Still warm in early autumn with reducing crowds. Often a comfortable time for sightseeing and outdoor activities across the Algarve.

Winter

Milder than the UK, quieter resorts and lower overall demand. Flight frequencies may reduce on some routes, so check current schedules carefully.

Faro Travel Guide

Faro is the capital of the Algarve and offers a mix of historic charm, coastal scenery and easy access to the wider region. The walled Old Town (Cidade Velha) is a highlight, with narrow streets, the cathedral and the Arco da Vila gateway.

Nearby, the Ria Formosa Natural Park provides lagoons, islands and boat trips. The marina area and surrounding streets offer restaurants serving fresh seafood and traditional Portuguese dishes.

Many visitors use Faro as a base or as a convenient arrival point before continuing to beach resorts along the Algarve coastline.

Explore the Algarve from Faro

Faro Airport is the main gateway to the Algarve. Popular destinations accessible by road transfer include Albufeira, Vilamoura, Lagos, Portimão, Tavira and Loulé. Distances and transfer times vary, so it is useful to plan your onward journey when choosing flight arrival times.

Albufeira

One of the best-known resort towns, popular for beaches and lively atmosphere.

Vilamoura

Marina, golf and a more upscale resort feel on the central Algarve coast.

Lagos

Historic town with dramatic cliffs, beaches and a vibrant old centre further west.

Tavira

Charming town on the eastern Algarve with a more relaxed atmosphere and nearby islands.
