The city

Why Marrakech?

Marrakech draws UK travellers with its medina, riads, souks, rooftop views and easy access to the Atlas Mountains. The city’s atmosphere — from Jemaa el-Fnaa to quiet courtyard stays — makes the flight from London or other UK airports a popular choice for short breaks and longer escapes.

Whether you come for culture, food or a change of pace, the journey begins with the right flight from the UK.

London to Marrakech flight information

Airport: Marrakech Menara (RAK), about 6 km from the city centre. Typical transfer 15–25 minutes.

Flight time: Non-stop services are usually around 3 hours 35 minutes to 4 hours. Connecting flights take longer.

Direct flights: Available from selected UK airports depending on airline and season. Always check current schedules for your dates.

Comparing fares: Look at different UK airports, flexible dates, baggage rules and total journey time — not only the base price.
